## 2:30-2:45 - the governance beat, as built

Ten seconds, one action, no narration over dead screen. From the office view, change the
identity in the masthead to the property master and let the check re-run.

What changes on screen, all of it enforced server-side:

- the strip under the revision line reads **PROPERTY MASTER - scoped to props - verbatim
  pages withheld**
- every quoted line in the evidence becomes `[redacted - full pages are access-controlled]`,
  and the scene and line beside it stay, so the finding still cites
- the ClickHouse panel keeps the query and says **rows returned - withheld**
- **Mark intentional** becomes **Office sign-off required**
- the drawer's Access tab shows who holds what, and the audit trail showing that the
  property master just looked

Say, once: *the policy is code, the roster is data, and every read is recorded.* Then
switch back. Do not linger - this beat is worth ten seconds and no more.

The line to have ready if a judge asks how it authenticates: **it does not.** It reads the
identity Cloud IAP already proved, on the header IAP already sets. There is no password
field anywhere in the repo.
